# CrPC to BNSS section mapping

The Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS) replaced the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (CrPC) from 1 July 2024. Official correspondence table of the new criminal laws. Confirm on the bare act before citing in court.
